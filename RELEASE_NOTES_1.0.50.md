# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.50 · Safeer Control 2.1.6 · Safeer OS 0.4.8

**Safeer OS 0.4.8**
- **Hide the side bar:** a new *Hide sidebar* button at the bottom of the side bar gives the whole screen to the page. A green handle on the left edge or Ctrl+B brings it back, and your choice is remembered.

**Security**
- A device you remove from your circle of trust loses access to your shared files at once, even in the middle of a video (before, its access token kept working for up to 12 hours).
- The local relay port for Global Link accepts only programs of your own user account; other users of the same computer cannot use it.

Safeer Browser 1.0.50 has no changes to the browser itself.

Slovensko: **Safeer OS 0.4.8.** Stransko vrstico lahko povsem skriješ z gumbom *Skrij vrstico*; vrne jo zeleni ročaj na levem robu ali Ctrl+B, izbira se zapomni. Varnost: naprava, ki jo odstraniš iz kroga zaupanja, takoj izgubi dostop do deljenih datotek (prej je žeton veljal še do 12 ur); lokalna vrata releja za Global Link sprejmejo samo programe tvojega uporabnika.
