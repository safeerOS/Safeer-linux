# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.53 · Safeer Control 2.1.9 · Safeer OS 0.4.11

**Safeer Link – every device says how much it can help (Safeer Control 2.1.9)**
- The computer reports its spare capacity to the other devices: processor, free memory and disk, graphics (hardware video encoding) and – on a laptop – battery.
- **When it helps:** only when it is not very busy, has enough memory and space, and on a laptop is plugged in or has at least 40 % battery. Below 30 % it stops and helps again from 40 % (so it does not flip back and forth at the limit). Streaming torrents for the TV follows the same rule.
- Phones, tablets and TVs with Safeer OS 0.5.15 report the same way, so *Safeer Link → Connected devices* shows for every device whether it can help and why not.

Safeer Browser 1.0.53 and Safeer OS 0.4.11 have no other changes.

Slovensko: **Vsaka naprava pove, koliko lahko pomaga:** računalnik drugim napravam sporoča prosto moč (procesor, pomnilnik, prostor, grafika, baterija prenosnika). Pomaga le, če ni preveč zaseden, ima pomnilnik in prostor ter je prenosnik na polnilcu ali ima vsaj 40 % baterije; pod 30 % neha. Enako velja za pretakanje torrentov za TV.
