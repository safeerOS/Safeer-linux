# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.88 · Safeer Control 2.1.44 · Safeer OS 0.4.46

## Safeer OS 0.4.46

- **One search field on the desktop finds more.** The search on the Safeer desktop now also finds Safeer OS itself (type "shield", "notes" or "devices" and that section is listed; "ads" or "dns" leads straight to the Shield), your notes (by title or text – choosing one opens that note) and the devices in your Safeer Link (by name, or by kind: "phone", "tv"). Choosing a device shows its files right there in the Files panel – or its apps, if it does not share files.
- **You no longer have to type č, š, ž.** "nocna osvetlitev" finds Nočna osvetlitev, "zur" finds Žur.mp4. Several words can be typed in any order; all of them must match. The same rules now apply everywhere: apps, files, notes, the media library, the filters in the Files and Apps panels, and jumping to a file by typing its first letters.
- **From the desktop into the Media Centre.** The last row of every search opens the Media Centre with what you typed. The catalogue is searched only there, once you choose it – nothing is sent to any source while you are typing on the desktop.
- **Enter still does what you expect.** An app that matches wins (as in a start menu), then Safeer OS, then a device found by its name; otherwise the first row – the web – stays selected. Shift+Enter always searches the web.

Safeer Browser 1.0.88 and Safeer Control 2.1.44 have no changes of their own (same code, new number).

Slovensko: **Eno iskalno polje na namizju najde več.** Iskanje na Safeerjevi delovni površini zdaj najde tudi Safeer OS sam (vtipkaj »scit«, »zapiski« ali »naprave« in razdelek je med zadetki; »oglasi« ali »dns« pelje naravnost k Ščitu), tvoje zapiske (po naslovu ali besedilu – izbira odpre ta zapisek) in naprave v tvojem Safeer Linku (po imenu ali po vrsti: »telefon«, »tv«). Izbira naprave pokaže njene datoteke kar v plošči Datoteke – ali njene programe, če datotek ne deli. **Šumnikov ni več treba tipkati.** »nocna osvetlitev« najde Nočno osvetlitev, »zur« najde Žur.mp4. Več besed lahko vtipkaš v poljubnem vrstnem redu, ujemati se morajo vse. Ista pravila zdaj veljajo povsod: programi, datoteke, zapiski, medijska knjižnica, filtra v ploščah Datoteke in Programi ter skok na datoteko s tipkanjem prvih črk. **Z namizja v Medijski center.** Zadnja vrstica vsakega iskanja odpre Medijski center z vtipkanim. Katalog se išče šele tam, ko to izbereš – med tipkanjem na namizju se nobenemu viru nič ne pošlje. **Enter še vedno naredi pričakovano.** Zmaga program, ki se ujema (kot v meniju Start), nato Safeer OS, nato naprava, najdena po imenu; sicer ostane izbrana prva vrstica – splet. Shift+Enter vedno išče po spletu. Safeer Browser 1.0.88 in Safeer Control 2.1.44 nimata svojih sprememb.
