# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.73 · Safeer Control 2.1.29 · Safeer OS 0.4.31

- **The computer remembers what it downloaded for your devices – with title and poster.** When the TV, tablet or phone hands a film from a torrent to the computer, it now also tells it the title and poster from the add-on. Safeer OS on your devices (0.5.43) shows these films on the shelf “On your devices” on every device in your Safeer Link – with a proper title and poster instead of the raw torrent name – and plays them from the computer's disk.
- **Private stays with the device that asked.** A title from a private add-on reaches the computer without title or poster, only marked as private. The computer tells about such a download only the device that asked for it; other devices do not see it on the shelf.
- A download that the computer has already removed (after 48 hours without playback) is also forgotten in this record.

Safeer Browser 1.0.73 and Safeer OS 0.4.31 have no changes of their own (same code, new number).

Slovensko: računalnik si zdaj zapomni, kaj je prenesel za tvoje naprave – z naslovom in plakatom, ki mu ju pove naprava. Safeer OS na napravah (0.5.43) te filme pokaže na polici »Na tvojih napravah« na vseh napravah v tvojem Safeer Linku, s pravim naslovom in plakatom namesto surovega imena torrenta, predvaja pa jih z diska računalnika. Naslov iz zasebnega dodatka pride do računalnika brez naslova in plakata, samo z oznako, da je zaseben; računalnik ga pove samo napravi, ki ga je prosila.
