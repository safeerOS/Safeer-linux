# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.71 · Safeer Control 2.1.27 · Safeer OS 0.4.29

- **Downloads for your other devices clean up after themselves.** When the computer streams a torrent for your TV, tablet or phone, it keeps the film in `Downloads/Safeer/Za naprave`. From now on a film that nobody has played for 48 hours is removed automatically, together with its files – so a film you paused is still there the next evening, and the disk does not fill up. When there is not enough room for a new film, the ones unused the longest go first (never one that was played in the last few hours). Files you share yourself are never touched.
- **Safeer Control no longer accumulates background threads.** Every search for a Safeer Link hub on the network left one thread behind; after many hours there were thousands. The search now cleans up after itself.

Safeer Browser 1.0.71 and Safeer OS 0.4.29 have no changes of their own (same code, new number).

Slovensko: film, ki ga računalnik prenese in pretaka za televizor, tablico ali telefon, se zdaj odstrani sam (z datotekami), ko ga 48 ur nihče ni predvajal; ko za nov film zmanjka prostora, gredo najprej tisti, ki jih najdlje nihče ni gledal. Datotek, ki jih deliš sam, se Safeer ne dotika. Safeer Control ne kopiči več niti v ozadju (iskanje središča Safeer Link je za sabo puščalo po eno nit).
