# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.47 · Safeer Control 2.1.3 · Safeer OS 0.4.5

**Safeer OS 0.4.5 – Messages.** A new *Messages* section brings your conversations together: one list, one conversation per person.

- **Email:** add an IMAP/SMTP account (Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, GMX, Siol, T-2 and others; servers are filled in from the address). The sign-in is checked before the account is added, and any error is shown right in the dialog. Reading in Safeer OS does not mark mail as read on the server, only new mail is downloaded, and replies keep the thread (*Re:*). Passwords are stored only in the system keyring (libsecret).
- **Chatwoot:** connect a Chatwoot inbox with its official API (https only).
- **Safeer Chat:** write to your own devices in Safeer Link – phone, tablet, TV or another computer. Messages travel only through your Safeer Link, never through a cloud. If a device is offline, the hub keeps the message for up to 7 days and delivers it when the device connects. Incoming messages show a desktop notification.
- **Search** finds people and messages; an open conversation updates by itself.
- **Safeer Control** now uses the language of your session (it was in English when Safeer Browser had no language set).
- **Fix:** the Safeer Control package now includes everything needed to receive messages.

Outlook and Hotmail require Microsoft sign-in (OAuth), which is not supported yet – the dialog says so.

Slovensko: **Safeer OS 0.4.5 – Sporočila.** Nov razdelek združi pogovore: en seznam, en pogovor na osebo. E-pošta (IMAP/SMTP, strežniki se izpolnijo sami, prijava se preveri pred dodajanjem, branje ne označi pošte kot prebrane, gesla samo v sistemski zbirki), Chatwoot prek uradnega API-ja in **Safeer Chat** med tvojimi napravami v Safeer Linku – brez oblaka; če naprava ni povezana, središče sporočilo hrani do 7 dni. Safeer Control govori v jeziku seje. Outlook in Hotmail zahtevata Microsoftovo prijavo, ki je še ni.
