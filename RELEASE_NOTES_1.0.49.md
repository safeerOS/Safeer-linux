# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.49 · Safeer Control 2.1.5 · Safeer OS 0.4.7

**Safeer OS 0.4.7 – magnet links, subtitles, DVD and media from your other devices.**

**Safeer Media**
- **Magnet links:** open a magnet link and choose which files to download. A video starts playing while it is still downloading, and you can send it to your TV or phone. Nothing is shared on after the download ends. A link from a website or another app is only checked; nothing connects to the network until you press *Open*. Files that could be programs are marked, and you decide yourself whether to continue (*Continue anyway*) – Safeer never runs them.
- **Subtitles like in VLC:** subtitle files next to the video, in a *Subs* folder, inside a torrent or on your other devices are found automatically. Broken numbering, times and old character sets (Windows-1250) are fixed. Pick a language or turn subtitles off with the *Subtitles* button or the V key; your choice is remembered.
- **DVD:** play unprotected DVDs from an ISO image, a VIDEO_TS folder or the disc drive. Copy-protected (CSS) discs are not played – Safeer does not unlock anything.
- **Music and video from your other devices** play right away without copying, encrypted, at home and over Global Link.

**Safeer Link**
- **Global Link:** a device outside your home network is reached through link.safeer.si when a direct connection fails twice.

**Security**
- The download engine runs only on this computer, with its own password, and stops together with Safeer OS. Only public trackers are kept in a magnet link (no addresses in your home network).

Safeer Browser 1.0.49 has no changes to the browser itself.

Slovensko: **Safeer OS 0.4.7 – magnet povezave, podnapisi, DVD in mediji z drugih naprav.** Magnet povezavo odpreš, izbereš datoteke, video se predvaja že med prenosom in ga lahko pošlješ na TV ali telefon; po koncu prenosa nič ne deliš naprej. Povezava s spletne strani ali iz drugega programa se samo preveri – omrežje se vklopi šele, ko pritisneš *Odpri*. Datoteke, ki so morda programi, so označene; sam odločiš, ali vseeno nadaljuješ (*Vseeno nadaljuj*), Safeer jih nikoli ne zažene. Podnapisi kot v VLC: ob videu, v mapi *Subs*, v torrentu ali na drugih napravah; popravek številk, časov in kodiranja (Windows-1250); izbira jezika in izklop z gumbom *Podnapisi* ali tipko V, izbira se zapomni. DVD brez zaščite iz slike ISO, mape VIDEO_TS ali pogona; zaščitenih diskov (CSS) ne predvajamo. Glasba in video z drugih naprav takoj in šifrirano, doma in prek Global Linka.
