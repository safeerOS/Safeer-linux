# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.77 · Safeer Control 2.1.33 · Safeer OS 0.4.35 · Safeer Cinnamon 1.1.0

- **Safeer from power-on.** Until now the Safeer look began after you signed in. With Safeer Cinnamon 1.1.0 the boot screen (the Safeer mark on a calm dark blue background, with a spinner; the disk-password prompt too) and the login screen (Safeer wallpaper and theme) can match. Turn it on in Safeer OS → Settings → “Safeer from power-on”, from the menu entry of the same name, or with `safeer-cinnamon --od-vklopa`.
- **You decide, and you can go back.** Installing the package changes nothing. Turning it on asks for the administrator password, because the boot and login screen belong to the whole computer. Before the first change Safeer remembers what was there; “Restore the previous boot and login screen” (or `safeer-cinnamon --od-vklopa-izklopi`, or “Restore previous look”, or removing the package) puts back exactly that. Login-screen settings you changed yourself in the meantime in Mint's Login Window stay yours.
- The lock screen already follows your wallpaper and theme, so with Safeer Cinnamon on it needs no change.
- Linux Mint stays underneath: only the standard mechanisms are used (the Plymouth theme choice through `update-alternatives`, `update-initramfs`, and `/etc/lightdm/slick-greeter.conf`). On a system without Plymouth or without the slick-greeter login screen that part is simply left as it is.
- **Media keys and the sound applet control the Safeer OS player.** Until now the system did not know Safeer OS was playing anything: the play/pause key on the keyboard or headset did nothing, and Cinnamon's sound applet showed no player. The player now speaks MPRIS, the standard every Linux desktop uses: play, pause, next, previous and seeking work from the keyboard, from headsets and from the applet, which shows the title, the artist and the artwork. The commands take the same path as the buttons in the player window, so resume points and the queue behave the same. A stopped player disappears from the applet.
- Internal: a method of the page bridge was listed twice (the second silently replaced the first); a test now guards against duplicates.

Safeer Browser 1.0.77 and Safeer Control 2.1.33 have no changes of their own (same code, new number). 1.0.76 / Safeer OS 0.4.34 was tagged but never published; this release contains it.

Slovensko: **Safeer od vklopa** – doslej se je videz Safeer začel šele po prijavi. S Safeer Cinnamon 1.1.0 se mu lahko pridružita zagonski zaslon (znak Safeer na mirnem temno modrem ozadju z vrtečim lokom; tudi vnos gesla za disk) in prijavni zaslon (ozadje in tema Safeer). Vklopiš ga v Safeer OS → Nastavitve → »Safeer od vklopa«, z istoimenskim vnosom v meniju ali z ukazom `safeer-cinnamon --od-vklopa`. Namestitev paketa ne spremeni ničesar; vklop vpraša za skrbniško geslo, ker zagonski in prijavni zaslon pripadata celemu računalniku. Pred prvo spremembo si Safeer zapomni prejšnje stanje, »Vrni prejšnji zagonski in prijavni zaslon« (ali `safeer-cinnamon --od-vklopa-izklopi`, »Vrni prejšnji videz« ali odstranitev paketa) pa vrne natanko to; kar si medtem sam spremenil v Mintovem Prijavnem oknu, ostane tvoje. Zaklenjen zaslon že sledi tvojemu ozadju in temi. Pod Safeerjem ostane Linux Mint: uporabljeni so samo običajni mehanizmi (izbira teme Plymouth, update-initramfs, slick-greeter.conf). **Medijske tipke in zvočni applet upravljajo predvajalnik Safeer OS** – doslej sistem ni vedel, da Safeer OS kaj predvaja: tipka za premor na tipkovnici ali slušalkah ni naredila nič, Cinnamonov zvočni applet ni pokazal predvajalnika. Predvajalnik zdaj govori MPRIS (standard vseh namizij Linux): predvajaj, premor, naslednja, prejšnja in preskok delujejo s tipkovnice, s slušalk in iz appleta, ki pokaže naslov, izvajalca in sliko. Ustavljen predvajalnik iz appleta izgine.
