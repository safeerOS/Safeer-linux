# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.118 · Safeer Control 2.1.74 · Safeer OS 0.4.76

**Convert files on your computer.** In Files, right-click an image, a sound file or a document and choose *Convert to …*. Images (also HEIC and AVIF) become JPG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, BMP, GIF or PDF; sound becomes MP3, OGG, Opus, FLAC, M4A or WAV; documents, spreadsheets and presentations become PDF, DOCX, ODT, XLSX, ODS, PPTX and more. Everything happens on this computer with ImageMagick, ffmpeg and LibreOffice – nothing is uploaded. The original stays; the converted file is saved next to it.

**Large files through Safeer Link, without starting over.** Files now travel in pieces: if Wi-Fi drops halfway, sending continues where it stopped instead of from the beginning (measured with 1 GB cut off after 300 MB: 1026 MB over the network instead of 1326 MB). Receiving resumes too. The 4 GB limit is gone – only free space counts – and whole folders are sent with their subfolders.

**Films in English, Slovenian films in Slovenian.** When a film offers several streams, the Media center prefers one with English audio (Slovenian for a Slovenian film), also over a dubbed version in higher resolution – but never over a stream the device cannot play. When another device converts a film live, it now keeps that language too.

**Public-domain films by language and genre.** Choosing a language and a genre now asks the Internet Archive for exactly that, instead of showing "no content in this language".

**Screen sharing.** The picture is scaled on the graphics card where possible (much less processor load) and sessions are measured for smoother sharing. The graphics card check can no longer fill the memory.
