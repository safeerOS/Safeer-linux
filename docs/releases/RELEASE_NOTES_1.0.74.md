# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.74 · Safeer Control 2.1.30 · Safeer OS 0.4.32

- **The shelf “On your devices” is now in the Media Centre on the computer too.** Under Films, Series, Video and All content you see every film or episode that any device in your Safeer Link has downloaded from a torrent – this computer, another computer, a phone, a tablet or the TV – with the title and poster from the add-on. Click to play: the device that keeps the film streams it, nothing is downloaded again. The ✕ on the card removes the download from the device that keeps it (two clicks, no accidental deletes). The same shelf has been in Safeer OS for Android since 0.5.43.
- **A film you watched in Safeer OS on the computer is there for your other devices.** Safeer OS remembers the title and poster of what it downloaded for watching; once the film is fully on disk, Safeer Control offers it to the TV, tablet and phone and streams it from disk. A device can also remove it.
- **Private stays private.** A title that comes from an add-on that marks itself as private in its manifest is remembered without title or poster and never appears on the shelf – on this computer or on any other device. When the manifest cannot be read, the add-on is treated as private.
- **What you still watch does not expire.** A download made for watching is removed 48 hours after it was last played – also when it is played from disk or by another device (until now the clock ran from the last download request).
- **Opening a title no longer starts a download.** Since 0.4.30, just opening the details of a film that only torrents offer already began downloading it; now the download starts when you press Play.
- Fixed: right after the torrent engine starts it does not list its torrents yet; removing a film or the automatic clean-up could take that for “the torrent is gone”, forget the record and leave the files on disk for good. The record now stays until the engine really no longer has the torrent.
- Safeer Control's torrent engine (the one that streams to devices) now keeps its own DHT, so it no longer shares one DHT address with the engine of Safeer OS.

Safeer Browser 1.0.74 has no changes of its own (same code, new number).

Slovensko: polica »Na tvojih napravah« je zdaj tudi v Medijskem centru na računalniku (Filmi, Serije, Video, Vse vsebine): kar je iz torrenta prenesla katera koli naprava v tvojem Safeer Linku – ta ali drug računalnik, telefon, tablica, televizor – z naslovom in plakatom iz dodatka. Klik predvaja: pretaka naprava, ki film hrani, nič se ne prenaša znova; ✕ na kartici prenos odstrani z naprave, ki ga hrani (dva klika). Film, ki si ga gledal v Safeer OS na računalniku, vidijo in predvajajo tudi televizor, tablica in telefon – in ga lahko odstranijo. Naslov iz dodatka, ki se v manifestu sam označi kot zaseben, si računalnik zapomni brez naslova in plakata in ga ni na polici nikjer; kadar manifesta ni mogoče prebrati, velja dodatek kot zaseben. Prenos zaradi gledanja se odstrani 48 ur po zadnjem predvajanju – tudi, ko ga gledaš z diska ali ga gleda druga naprava. Odprte podrobnosti naslova ne začnejo več prenosa torrenta (prenos se začne šele ob Predvajaj). Popravljeno: takoj po zagonu motor torrentov svojih torrentov še ne našteje; odstranitev ali samodejno čiščenje je to lahko razumelo kot »torrenta ni več«, pozabilo zapis in datoteke pustilo na disku.
