# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.133 · Safeer OS 0.4.91 (with Safeer Control 2.1.88)

**Your devices, your inner circle.** Keys, tokens and sources with a password are shared only with devices in your inner circle of trust, encrypted, and never with other devices in Safeer Link. Removing a device from the circle immediately ends its sessions, tokens and files you sent it. Keys a service rejects or locks are not used again until you check them, to protect your account.

**Remote screen and files.** One switcher for this computer and the screens of your devices, live thumbnails, lossless image on a wired network, sharper text. Files of linked devices with thumbnails and large folders in parts; names and paths from another device can never leave the shared folder.

**Safeer Player as its own package.** The Media Centre alone, in its own window, as a separate .deb (safeer-player), without the Safeer OS desktop.

**Engine and stability.** Our WebKitGTK 2.54.2 with the hardened build, faster threat-list checks in Safeer Shield, and many stability fixes in the Media Centre and players.
