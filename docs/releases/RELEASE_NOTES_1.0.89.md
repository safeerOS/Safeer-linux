# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.89 · Safeer Control 2.1.45 · Safeer OS 0.4.47

## Safeer OS 0.4.47

- **Drop a file on a device to send it.** In Files on the Safeer desktop, drag files onto a device in the side list – or choose "Send to a device …" from the right-click menu, also for several selected files. The files stay where they are; the device gets a copy in its downloads folder, checked against its SHA-256. A message shows the progress and, if it does not work, says why (the device is not connected, it cannot be reached on this network, …). Folders are not sent.

## Safeer Control 2.1.45

- **Sending a file from the computer works now that every device is its own hub.** In Safeer Link each device runs its own hub and the hubs talk to each other. Messages travel across hubs, but a file handed to the computer's hub never reached a device on another hub: the device looked for it at its own. Safeer Control now hands the file to the hub of the receiving device (signing in with this computer's key from the trust ring, the same way hubs connect to each other), and a file sent to this computer is fetched from the sender's hub.
- **"Send text" and "Send file" no longer answer "Hub is not known".** A computer that is its own hub has no pairing token, and these two actions required one. They now sign in with the key from the trust ring, like everything else.
- **The hub on a computer can relay text and files.** Until now it only relayed messages: the two paths other devices use to send text and files through a hub (`/cast/share/text`, `/cast/file`) were missing and answered 404 and 501. They now behave as on the Android hub. A file waits in the user's cache folder for at most one hour, is written in pieces and never held in memory, and the number of simultaneous transfers is limited. Screen sharing through a computer hub is still not available.

Not yet: sending to a computer with an older Safeer Control (it answers that its hub cannot do this yet), and to a device that is away from home (reached only through Global Link).

Safeer Browser 1.0.89 has no changes of its own (same code, new number).

Slovensko: **Spusti datoteko na napravo in jo s tem pošlji.** V Datotekah na Safeerjevi delovni površini povlečeš datoteke na napravo v stranskem seznamu – ali izbereš »Pošlji na napravo …« v meniju desnega klika, tudi za več izbranih datotek. Datoteke ostanejo, kjer so; naprava dobi kopijo v svojo mapo prenosov, preverjeno s SHA-256. Sporočilo kaže napredek in, če ne gre, pove zakaj (naprava ni povezana, v tem omrežju ni dosegljiva …). Mape se ne pošiljajo. **Safeer Control 2.1.45 – pošiljanje datoteke z računalnika deluje tudi zdaj, ko je vsaka naprava svoje središče.** V Safeer Linku ima vsaka naprava svoje središče, središča pa se pogovarjajo med sabo. Sporočila gredo čez središča, datoteka, oddana središču računalnika, pa do naprave pri drugem središču ni prišla: naprava jo je iskala pri svojem. Safeer Control zdaj datoteko odda središču naprave, ki jo sprejme (prijavi se s ključem tega računalnika iz kroga zaupanja, enako kot se središča povezujejo med sabo), datoteko, poslano temu računalniku, pa prevzame pri središču pošiljatelja. **»Pošlji besedilo« in »Pošlji datoteko« ne odgovorita več »Hub ni znan«.** Računalnik, ki je sam svoje središče, nima žetona seznanitve, ti dejanji pa sta ga zahtevali; zdaj se prijavita s ključem iz kroga zaupanja kot vse drugo. **Središče na računalniku zna posredovati besedilo in datoteke.** Doslej je posredovalo samo sporočila: poti, po katerih druge naprave pošiljajo besedilo in datoteke prek središča (`/cast/share/text`, `/cast/file`), ni imelo in je odgovarjalo 404 in 501. Zdaj delujeta kot na središču na Androidu. Datoteka čaka v uporabnikovi mapi predpomnilnika največ eno uro, piše se po kosih in se nikoli ne drži v pomnilniku, število hkratnih prenosov je omejeno. Deljenja zaslona prek središča na računalniku še ni. Še ne gre: pošiljanje na računalnik s starejšim Safeer Controlom (odgovori, da njegovo središče tega še ne zna) in na napravo, ki ni doma (dosegljiva samo prek Global Linka). Safeer Browser 1.0.89 nima svojih sprememb.
