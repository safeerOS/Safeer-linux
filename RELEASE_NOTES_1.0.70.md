# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.70 · Safeer Control 2.1.26 · Safeer OS 0.4.28

- **Media Center with the same catalogue as on your other devices:** Films, Series, Music, Radio and Live TV as a poster grid with genres, sorting and search. A film or series opens its details (seasons, episodes, where to watch); the built-in legal sources – public-domain films, Jamendo music, radio stations, live TV – work without any setup. Your own folders and files stay where they were.
- **Add-ons and sources:** "Add-ons and sources" adds a Stremio add-on, your media server (Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, Navidrome, Kodi library, TVHeadend, DLNA/UPnP, Funkwhale, AzuraCast) or a web source (RSS, M3U). Safeer only lists what it can play itself, and never shows donation requests or Discord invites from add-ons. Server passwords are kept in the system keyring, not in a file.
- **Playlists:** paste a YouTube or Spotify playlist link and the tracks play in order (shuffle, repeat, previous/next); build your own playlist with the "+" on any track. The right-hand column shows what is playing and the queue.
- **Playlists are the same on all your devices** in the same Safeer Link – phone, tablet, TV and computers. They sync directly between the devices, with no account and no cloud; changes and deletions included. Other devices need Safeer OS 0.5.37 (Android) or 1.0.28 (Windows) or newer.
- A film from the catalogue continues where you stopped.
- Tracks from a playlist play in a separate sandboxed view; nothing from the web is embedded into the Safeer OS page itself.

Safeer Browser 1.0.70 has no changes to the browser itself.
