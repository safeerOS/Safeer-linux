# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.75 · Safeer Control 2.1.31 · Safeer OS 0.4.33

- **Keep.** A film on the shelf “On your devices” used to disappear 48 hours after it was last played. The bookmark in the corner of the poster now keeps it: a kept download does not expire and is not removed to make room for another film – only you remove it. The mark lives on the device that keeps the film, so it works for films on this computer, on another computer and on a phone, tablet or TV. A device that runs an older version is not offered the option.
- **Subtitles from the torrent when another device keeps the film.** When a phone, a TV or another computer streams a film to this computer, the subtitle files from the same torrent now come along and appear in the player's subtitle menu. Safeer Control offers them the other way round too: a TV or phone that watches a film this computer downloaded gets its subtitles.
- **Your sources are the same on the computer and on your Android devices.** An add-on, podcast, playlist or stream you add in the Media Centre on the computer appears on your phone, tablet and TV in Safeer Link – and what you add there appears here, as long as this computer can play it. Removing a source removes it everywhere; the ✕ now asks once more (“Remove from all devices?”) for such sources. Nothing goes to a cloud, only between your devices.
- **Private stays where you added it.** An add-on that marks itself as private in its manifest is never sent to other devices and never taken over from them. While Safeer cannot tell whether an add-on is private, it treats it as private.
- Web pages you saved as sources on Android (they open in the browser there) are not copied into the Media Centre on the computer; for pages the computer has Web apps.
- **Apps: this computer and favourites (Safeer Cinnamon desktop).** The Apps panel now lists the programs of this computer too – choose “This computer” in the device list, one click starts the program. Every tile has a star: click ☆ and the program is kept under ★ Favourites, which is now always the first tab (it used to be hidden until you had a favourite, so it was hard to find) and opens first once you have favourites. Web shortcuts are no longer listed as a device; they are under Web.

Safeer Browser 1.0.75 has no changes of its own (same code, new number).

Slovensko: **Obdrži** – film na polici »Na tvojih napravah« je doslej izginil 48 ur po zadnjem predvajanju; zaznamek v kotu plakata ga zdaj obdrži: obdržan prenos ne poteče in se ne umakne novemu filmu, odstraniš ga samo ti. Oznako hrani naprava, ki film hrani, zato velja za filme na tem računalniku, drugem računalniku, telefonu, tablici in televizorju; naprava s starejšo različico možnosti nima. **Podnapisi iz torrenta** pridejo tudi, kadar film temu računalniku pretaka druga naprava – in obratno: televizor ali telefon, ki gleda film s tega računalnika, dobi njegove podnapise. **Moji viri so enaki na računalniku in na napravah z Androidom**: dodatek, podkast, seznam ali tok, ki ga dodaš v Medijskem centru na računalniku, se pojavi na telefonu, tablici in televizorju v Safeer Linku – in obratno, če ga računalnik zna predvajati. Izbris velja povsod; ✕ pri takem viru zdaj vpraša še enkrat (»Odstrani z vseh naprav?«). Dodatek, ki se v manifestu sam označi kot zaseben, ostane na napravi, kjer si ga dodal. Nič ne gre v oblak, samo med tvojimi napravami. **Programi: ta računalnik in priljubljeni (namizje Safeer Cinnamon)** – plošča Programi zdaj pokaže tudi programe tega računalnika (v izbiri naprav »Ta računalnik«, en klik zažene program). Na vsaki ploščici je zvezdica: klik na ☆ program shrani med ★ Priljubljene, ki so zdaj vedno prvi zavihek (prej je bil skrit, dokler nisi imel priljubljenih, zato ga je bilo težko najti) in se odprejo prvi, ko jih imaš. Spletne bližnjice niso več naštete kot naprava; najdeš jih pod Splet.
