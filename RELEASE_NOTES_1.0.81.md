# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.81 · Safeer Control 2.1.37 · Safeer OS 0.4.39 · Safeer Cinnamon 1.2.0

## Safeer Cinnamon 1.2.0 (the optional look for Linux Mint Cinnamon)

- **You choose how much of it you want.** "Safeer Cinnamon" in the menu now opens a small window with three layouts: *Look only* (theme, icons and wallpaper – your panel, window list and desktop icons stay where they are), *Look and dock* (the panel moves to the top, a dock sits at the bottom) and *Workspace* (the dock plus the Safeer OS workspace instead of desktop icons). Nothing changes until you press Turn on, and "Restore previous look" puts everything back. Until now, turning it on did all of this at once.
- **One list of windows, not two.** In the layouts with a dock, the dock shows pinned and open programs, so the window list leaves the panel; the programs you had pinned there move into the dock. Going back to *Look only*, or turning Safeer Cinnamon off, returns the panel, the window list with its pinned programs and your own Plank dock exactly as they were.
- **The dock fits the screen.** Icon size follows the screen and the text size (36 px on a small laptop, 44 px at 1080p, up to 64 px with larger text) instead of a fixed 48 px; on a small screen the dock makes way for a window that would cover it. It sits on the screen edge, is less transparent, and the count badge of programs that report one is visible (it was transparent). Whatever you change in the dock settings stays yours, also when you switch layouts. In *Look and dock*, the dock is placed again when the screen resolution changes.
- **The theme is finished.** Mint's greys and saturated blue used to show through wherever the theme had no rule of its own: scrollbars, progress bars, tabs, toolbars, frames, shell dialogs, check boxes, radio buttons, switches, window buttons. They are now in the Safeer palette for GTK 3, GTK 4 and the Cinnamon shell, built from the Mint-Y themes of Linux Mint 22.0 to 22.3 so that it matches on each of them (checked on Cinnamon 6.2 and 6.6). The panel is less transparent, so its text stays readable over a bright wallpaper.
- **Contrast for programs too.** `safeer-cinnamon --kontrast` now changes the shell and the programs: white text, stronger borders, a solid focus ring. `--vecji-tekst 150` adds a 150 % step.
- **Your wallpaper stays yours.** The window offers your own wallpaper next to the four Safeer wallpapers, and a wallpaper you pick while Safeer Cinnamon is on is remembered for the next time. When you pick a Safeer wallpaper, a running wallpaper slideshow is paused (it would replace the picture at once) and resumes when you turn Safeer Cinnamon off.
- **Removing the package restores your look.** Uninstalling safeer-cinnamon first returns the previous look to every user who had it on (it used to print a reminder only).
- **Installing with GDebi brings the dock.** Plank is now a dependency (GDebi does not install recommended packages). The Papirus icons stay a recommendation: without them the theme uses Mint's blue icons.
- **Upgrading from 1.1:** at the first login the layout you had is kept. The window list moves from the panel into the dock (the programs pinned on the panel are added to your dock), and the size of your dock icons is left as you set it. Open "Safeer Cinnamon" in the menu to pick another layout.

## Safeer OS 0.4.39

- **One switch for larger text and less transparency.** On the Safeer Cinnamon workspace, "Larger text" and "Less transparency" now drive the system settings – the text size and the contrast theme of the shell and of programs – and follow them when you change these elsewhere. They used to keep their own copies that did not match the system.

Safeer Browser 1.0.81 and Safeer Control 2.1.37 have no changes of their own (same code, new number).
