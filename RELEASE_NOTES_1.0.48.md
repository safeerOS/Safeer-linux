# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.48 · Safeer Control 2.1.4 · Safeer OS 0.4.6

**Safeer OS 0.4.6 – Safeer Media and a Safeer Link without a single hub.**

**Safeer Media**
- **Your own library:** add folders with music, videos and photos – files stay where they are, nothing is copied or uploaded. *Rescan* finds new files and forgets deleted ones; a disconnected drive keeps its entries. *Media folders* lists your folders; removing one only hides it from the library, your files are untouched.
- **Music:** a song plays its whole folder as an album, with next and previous on every track, and keeps playing in the background with a bar at the bottom. The library is sorted A–Z.
- **Video:** built-in player in a large window, full screen with the button, F11 or a double click, and it resumes where you stopped. Keys: Space, ←/→, N/P, F/F11, Esc. The player follows the interface language.
- **Photos:** a built-in gallery with thumbnails, arrow keys and click to zoom.
- **TV and radio:** add a direct stream you are allowed to play (for example your public broadcaster's radio). A stream that does not work shows a clear message. Suggested services open the provider's website and are labelled *Website* – Safeer never presents a website as a direct stream.
- The *Sources and apps* row shows only media players (VLC, Celluloid, Rhythmbox …), not image editors or scanners.

**Safeer Link – decentralized**
- Every device hosts its own hub and connects directly to your other devices; there is no single hub any more. If two devices cannot reach each other, a third one passes the message on (one hop).
- Only devices from your signed circle of trust can join. Devices remember each other's addresses and reconnect about 2 seconds after a restart.

**Also new**
- **Speakers on your network:** send the computer's sound to a DLNA speaker (for example a JBL soundbar) from the *Sound* page.
- **Web inside Safeer OS:** the built-in browser with tabs, the same as on Windows.
- **Side bar:** collapses to icons with a button or Ctrl+B, and stays that way.
- **Privacy:** the local messages database is readable only by you.

Safeer Browser 1.0.48 has no changes to the browser itself.
