# Safeer Browser for Linux 1.0.94 · Safeer Control 2.1.50 · Safeer OS 0.4.52

## Safeer Link (Safeer Control 2.1.50, Safeer OS 0.4.52)

- **The hub defends itself.** Every device with Safeer has its own port in the home network, through which your other devices connect. Whoever is not in your circle has no business there — but until now a device in the same network (a guest, an unknown device, an infected smart gadget) could probe the port, keep signing in with refused signatures and start pairing over and over, without end; nobody counted. The hub now counts hostile attempts per device. A device that exceeds the threshold is stopped for 10 minutes, twice as long at every repetition (up to one hour): its connection is closed at once, with no answer and without the certificate of the computer.
- **Guessing the pairing code is limited.** The code has six digits and one sign-in allows five guesses — but the number of sign-ins was unlimited, so a device in the same network could try several hundred codes an hour for as long as it liked. The hub now counts all guesses together, whatever address they come from: after 20 unsuccessful guesses within an hour pairing by code is closed — for one hour the first time, one day the second, one week every time after that. An attacker that never gives up gets fewer than 250 guesses in a year, out of 900,000 possible codes. The state survives a restart.
- **Your own devices keep working.** A device signed in to your circle is never taken for an attacker because of many connections (browsing a folder of pictures opens hundreds), and the recognition every device performs before it connects is not counted. Neither is your own pairing: a successful sign-in gives its guess back. While pairing by code is closed, you add a device by opening "Connect devices" on one of your devices, or with the QR code (which cannot be guessed).
- **You are told.** A desktop notification says which device was stopped, why and for how long; when pairing by code closes, it says from which addresses somebody was guessing and how to add a device meanwhile. If the hub knows a device or a neighbouring hub at that address by name, the notification names it — then it is most likely your own device with a broken sign-in. A device that tries to join during a closure says why it cannot (a device with an older version shows its general message).
- **Notifications of Safeer Link no longer need `notify-send`.** They go to the desktop over D-Bus. `notify-send` (package `libnotify-bin`) is not a dependency of the packages; on a computer without it the notification with the **pairing code** was not shown at all, so a new device could not be connected from that computer's screen.
- **The notification with the pairing code follows the language of the session.** It was always Slovenian.

Verified: with tests of the rules, with a real hub on the loopback and over a real network against a hub in a test container (a patient attacker with one sign-in and five guesses every 61 seconds stayed below the per-device threshold and was stopped by the guard at the twentieth guess; the desktop notification appeared).

Known limits: there is no list of stopped devices and no state of pairing by code in the interface yet — a block ends by itself. Every device has its own guard, so a circle of five devices gives a patient attacker five times as many guesses. Somebody in the same network can keep pairing by code closed for everyone ("Connect devices" on your device and the QR code keep working); once the ten guesses allowed with "Connect devices" open are used up during a closure, the code that screen shows is refused until the closure ends — the screen does not say so yet. The limits are chosen from the protocol, not measured in households. The advert of a computer in the network (name, fingerprint) is still visible to everyone in it; a hidden advert follows in the next releases. Windows does not have the defence yet.

Slovensko: **Središče Safeer Linka se brani.** Vsak računalnik s Safeerjem ima v domačem omrežju svoja vrata, skozi katera se povezujejo tvoje druge naprave. Doslej je lahko naprava v istem omrežju brez konca tipala po vratih, se prijavljala z zavrnjenimi podpisi in znova in znova začenjala povezovanje – nihče tega ni štel. Zdaj središče sovražne poskuse šteje po napravi: kdor preseže mejo, je ustavljen za 10 minut, ob ponovitvi dvakrat dlje (do ene ure) – povezava se mu zapre takoj, brez odgovora in brez potrdila računalnika. **Ugibanje kode za povezavo je omejeno.** Koda ima šest številk in ena prijava dovoli pet poskusov, prijav pa je bilo lahko poljubno – naprava v istem omrežju je lahko poskusila več sto kod na uro, kolikor dolgo je hotela. Zdaj središče šteje vse poskuse skupaj, ne glede na naslov: po 20 neuspelih poskusih v eni uri se povezovanje s kodo zapre – prvič za eno uro, drugič za en dan, potem vsakič za en teden. Kdor nikoli ne odneha, v enem letu ne pride niti do 250 poskusov od 900.000 možnih kod. Stanje preživi ponovni zagon. **Tvoje naprave delajo naprej:** prijavljena naprava ni nikoli šteta za napadalca zaradi veliko povezav, prepoznava središča ne šteje, tvoje povezovanje tudi ne (uspešna prijava svoj poskus vrne). Dokler je povezovanje s kodo zaprto, novo napravo dodaš tako, da na eni od svojih naprav odpreš »Poveži naprave«, ali s kodo QR. **Obvestilo** na namizju pove, katera naprava je bila ustavljena, zakaj in za koliko časa, ob zapori kode pa, s katerih naslovov je kdo ugibal in kako zdaj dodaš napravo; znano napravo ali sosednje središče imenuje. **Obvestila Safeer Linka ne potrebujejo več orodja `notify-send`** (paket ga ne zahteva): na računalniku brez njega se obvestilo s **kodo za povezavo** sploh ni pokazalo. Obvestilo s kodo zdaj sledi jeziku seje (prej vedno slovensko). Znane omejitve: seznama ustavljenih naprav in stanja povezovanja s kodo v vmesniku še ni; vsaka naprava ima svojo varovalko (v krogu petih naprav ima vztrajen napadalec petkrat toliko poskusov); kdor je v istem omrežju, lahko povezovanje s kodo drži zaprto (»Poveži naprave« in koda QR delata naprej); meje so določene iz protokola, ne izmerjene v gospodinjstvih; oglas računalnika v omrežju (ime, odtis) je še viden vsem v njem; Windows obrambe še nima.
